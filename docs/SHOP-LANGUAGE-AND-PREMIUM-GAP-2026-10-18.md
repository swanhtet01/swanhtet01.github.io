## Where the product still misses the market

1. **First-use and speed:** ZayTap's marketed add → stock → sell → report path is easy to explain. Shop has more controls but still needs a measured first-run test: create/import five real MMK items, enter opening stock, scan and sell, find the receipt, restock, and read the daily result. Acceptance: an untrained Myanmar operator completes it without staff intervention; record time, clicks, errors and recovery. Keep advanced policy/accounting behind a deliberate entry.
2. **Supported hardware:** Square's scanner/printer guidance names connection modes and compatibility. Shop's keyboard-wedge/camera code and browser-print output are not a supported-device matrix. Test one Windows USB-HID scanner, one Android camera/OTG path, and a named thermal receipt/label printer with Burmese glyphs. Record OS/browser/device, repeated scans, paper size, disconnect/reconnect and fallback. Until then, say "browser print" rather than claiming printer integration.
3. **Inventory truth:** Square documents reasoned stock adjustment, purchase receiving, location history and barcode labels. Shop has source-level receiving/counting/label actions, but needs a tested end-to-end stock ledger with partial receipt, damaged goods, returns, unit conversion, stale-count conflict and audit trail. Show the next useful stock action, not a wall of controls.
4. **Channel orders:** Autumn markets automatic social intake. A pasted message or manually logged request does not meet that bar. A first connector needs owner-authorized provider access, source event ID, dedupe, customer consent, item match/review, stock reservation, retries/dead-letter visibility and a clear human exception path. Do not call it automatic before a real provider event reaches Shop end-to-end.
5. **Managed trust:** Local screenshots and green typechecks do not prove managed account isolation, migration health, durable order writes, backup restore, or safe recovery after interrupted checkout. Run two-tenant hosted acceptance and a disconnection/retry test on the exact candidate. Keep commercial access closed until those pass.
6. **Website proof:** Distinguish the company story from Shop conversion. Show legible real application captures, tested feature claims, Myanmar use cases, pricing/support/terms and a clear trial path. The production domain must match the actual shipped UI and Shop-only access; review-only captures and vendor-inspired artwork cannot substitute.
