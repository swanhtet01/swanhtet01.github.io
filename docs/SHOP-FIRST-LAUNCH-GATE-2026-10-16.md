## REA and benchmark: how they change the build

REA is a research instrument for bounded evidence gathering, not an automatic product builder. Use it on operator-supplied binaries, packages, or passive runtime targets; keep observations, vendor claims, design inferences, and SuperMega tests in separate evidence lanes. Convert confirmed customer workflows into our own clean implementation and regression tests; do not copy proprietary source, assets, or undocumented internals.

The current benchmark supports these next product decisions:

1. Finish catalog categories and a validated import preview so counter setup stays fast while product identity, barcode, stock, and price remain reviewable.
2. Validate scanner and receipt-printer support against an explicit Myanmar-relevant device/browser matrix; retain keyboard/manual fallback and never market untested hardware as compatible.
3. Keep channel orders behind explicit connectors, authorization, deduplication, stock reservation, reconciliation, and an audit trail. Do not turn pasted chat text into an order automatically.
4. Keep Myanmar-language setup/receipts and clear Free/Paid boundaries grounded in implemented behavior and support capacity, not marketing claims.
5. Refresh the source-backed competitor ledger when a testable first-party artifact, product page, or hardware path is available; mark unavailable coverage instead of inferring behavior.
