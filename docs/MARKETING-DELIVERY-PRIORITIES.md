## Positioning

SuperMega prepares business tools with the customer. Shop is the primary
operating product; Website and Ecommerce are assisted delivery services.
Customer effort is a short business brief and review of the prepared result.
Do not sell a builder, an autonomous company, or unverified enterprise readiness.
Plant is excluded from acquisition, navigation and sitemap. Preserve existing
records and retained-access compatibility; this is not a data-deletion project.

Keep the existing SuperMega mark, jade accent, warm neutral surfaces, dark ink,
system typography and accessible focus. Do not create a separate identity for
every route. Public pages explain outcomes; app pages show the next operating
job; internal tools show decisions, exceptions and evidence.

## Bounded experiments

Use prototypes or explicit participant consent before changing live journeys.
No fabricated A/B evidence and no thousands of unattended iterations.

| Hypothesis | Task | Decision evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assisted language is clearer than builder language | Identify the right offer and explain the next step | At least 4 of 5 target users understand without operator explanation; record confusion and limitations |
| A short brief is enough to begin | Submit a synthetic business enquiry | Completion time, validation failures and unnecessary fields; verify no false receipt |
| Trade-specific Shop entry improves first use | Find item, review manual tender, recover a held sale on tablet | Task completion, wrong actions and recovery; zero unexplained money variance |
| Prepared output is valuable | Review Website contact action or Ecommerce request | User can verify business facts and request a correction without editing tools |
