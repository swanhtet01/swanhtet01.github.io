# Ecommerce — operator walkthrough

## 1. Client and the 30-second pitch

Current public page:

- `Ecommerce | Your product catalog | SuperMega`
- `Help customers browse your products and prepare a request. Your team confirms availability, orders and payment.`

The public website shows interface screenshots and one Login. Contact is in the footer. It has no sample, trial, setup or product-launch buttons.

Use this as a service walkthrough. The product route is not proof of a provisioned business, published deliverable or completed customer acceptance.

## 2. Pre-demo setup

Use an isolated browser profile and synthetic business details. Customer entry starts at `/ecommerce/` or `Login`; it does not require customers to configure a sample workspace. Internal legacy setup routes are retained for rehearsals only. Never reset existing customer work for a presentation.

## 3. Demo script

1. Open `/ecommerce/` without a saved workspace override. Verify the business brief is shown for an unsigned visitor.
2. Enter a fictional business name and concise requirements. Use only facts supplied for the exercise; leave unknown details unresolved.
3. Use the optional reference field when there is a relevant source. Check that entered facts remain intact before continuing.
4. Choose `Continue` and verify that the handoff preserves the product and brief. Stop before sending customer details unless that submission is authorized.
5. For an existing local workspace, return through app home and open its saved product card. The `Local workspace` label means device storage, not managed persistence. Check saved content rather than creating another sample.
6. For a managed delivery, verify the assigned account, durable saved content and review outcome separately. Record the exact release tested; do not turn a local walkthrough into a hosted acceptance claim.

## 4. Objection handling: the boundary

Pricing and service scope require an agreed quote; these instructions promise no free tier or live account. No payment, stock movement, publication or customer message occurs as part of this walkthrough. A prepared draft or request receipt is not a published deliverable. Backups, isolation and recovery need their own acceptance evidence.

## 5. The close

The historical `Request assisted setup` route remains `/contact/?product=ecommerce`. Confirm the intended business facts and contact details before an authorized submission. Retain the returned request reference, then track preparation, customer review and delivery as separate outcomes. Avoid asking customers to operate the internal builder or legacy sample setup.
