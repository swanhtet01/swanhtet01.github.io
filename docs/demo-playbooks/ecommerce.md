# Commerce — operator walkthrough

## 1. Client and the 30-second pitch

Current public page:

- `Commerce | Your product catalog | SuperMega`
- `The SuperMega team manages the Shop-backed catalog and reviews customer requests before any order or payment is confirmed.`

The public website shows interface screenshots and one Login. Contact is in the footer. It has no sample, trial, setup or product-launch buttons.

Use this as a service walkthrough. The product route is not proof of a provisioned business, published deliverable or completed customer acceptance.

## 2. Pre-demo setup

Use an isolated browser profile and synthetic business details. The public product page is `/ecommerce/`; `Login` is the customer entry for Shop. Our team manages Commerce, while the public storefront and customer review remain available through their own routes. Internal legacy setup routes are retained for rehearsals only. Never reset existing customer work for a presentation.

## 3. Demo script

1. Open `/ecommerce/` as an unsigned visitor. Show the current product description and distinct catalog and request screenshots without claiming that the public page is a customer management console.
2. In an authorized internal test workspace, show the actual Shop-backed catalog and confirm product availability from synthetic records.
3. Open the public storefront with synthetic items. Show that a customer request is separate from a confirmed Shop order and payment.
4. In the internal Commerce view, review that request and its next decision without recording a sale or contacting a customer.
5. Reopen the saved local request. The `Local workspace` label means device storage, not managed persistence; check the saved content rather than creating another sample.
6. For a managed delivery, verify team access, the public storefront, customer review and durable request-to-Shop handoff separately. Record the exact release tested; do not turn a local walkthrough into a hosted acceptance claim.

## 4. Objection handling: the boundary

Pricing and service scope require an agreed quote; these instructions promise no free tier or live account. No payment, stock movement, publication or customer message occurs as part of this walkthrough. A prepared draft or request receipt is not a published deliverable. Backups, isolation and recovery need their own acceptance evidence.

## 5. The close

The historical `Request assisted setup` route remains `/contact/?product=ecommerce`. Confirm the intended business facts and contact details before an authorized submission. Retain the returned request reference, then track preparation, customer review and delivery as separate outcomes. Avoid asking customers to operate the internal builder or legacy sample setup.
