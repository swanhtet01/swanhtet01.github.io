## Applied to the Shop candidate

The benchmark’s safety-critical scan rule is now implemented in source: exact SKU/barcode lookup produces a sale item only when exactly one catalog record matches. Duplicate matches are surfaced as **ambiguous** and require correction; misses remain distinct and may enter the existing explicit create-review path. A scan never creates a product or commits a sale. This keeps the one-scan fast path while preventing the wrong item from silently entering checkout.

Implementation: `showroom/src/core/commerce-workspace.ts` (`resolveCommerceItemByScanCode`) and `showroom/src/core/CoreApp.tsx` (`addScannedValue`). Regression coverage: `tools/test_barcode_scan_boundary.mjs` and `tools/shop_unmatched_scan.test.mjs`.

Focused evidence on this candidate: `npm run app:typecheck` passed; the barcode boundary suite passed 138 checks; unmatched-scan tests passed 3/3. These are local source/test results only. They do not establish Android hardware compatibility, a connected scanner/printer, hosted auth/tenant isolation, or customer acceptance.
