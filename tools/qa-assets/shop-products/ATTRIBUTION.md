# Synthetic Shop product-photo source

The `Red apple (each)` capture fixture uses the original, unmodified
[`Red apple on a plate 2017 A.jpg`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_apple_on_a_plate_2017_A.jpg)
by **Fructibus**. The Commons file page says it is the uploader's own work,
dedicated under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The 4,494 × 3,371 JPEG is stored as `red-apple-each.jpg` without a crop or
re-encode; SHA-256:
`4de1eb5c433f10867327d8b6303bfc45635028e8c0eb416f167f10ddf3dbdf08`.
This is an unbranded, single red apple, matching the fixture product name.
`MMK 1,500` is an illustrative fixture amount, not a merchant or live price.
Credit Fructibus and link this file page in any public display even though CC0
does not require attribution. The source check is a candidate, not an owner
attestation for the generated gallery.

The MAMA Chicken pack photo has been removed from the current fixture and
source tree. The [Open Food Facts license guidance](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/)
licenses contributor photography but warns that packaging artwork may carry
separate rights. The prior MAMA asset must not be reused without those rights
being reviewed. Older commits and gallery PNGs may still contain it; only the
new source-bound capture candidate should be considered for review.

The four retailer-sourced photos had no verified commercial reuse grant and
remain excluded from the fixture and current source tree. Their listings in
`sources.json` are item references only. The retailer's
[terms, section 3.1](https://www.makropro.com.mm/en/static/terms-conditions)
reserve copying and public display absent written consent. No product listing
or illustrative price is an image-use license. Public publication still needs
the exact capture review and rights attestation; this ledger does not waive
that gate.
