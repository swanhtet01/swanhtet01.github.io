# SUPERMEGA.dev Design Control

This file is the source of truth for public website, business card, video, and social assets.

## Brand Position

SUPERMEGA.dev builds custom business software from one real workflow.

Start with a visible result: a work screen, a clean record set, or an owner brief. Expand only after the first screen is useful.

## Voice

- Short, direct, practical.
- Show the product before explaining the platform.
- Say what the client sends, what we build, and what they get back.
- Avoid sci-fi language, fake dashboards, and broad AI claims without a visible workflow.

## Core Line (CEO-ratified 2026-06-25)

**Stop running your business on Viber & Excel.**

Plain language, sets a clear expectation. Used everywhere (hero, page titles, social). Do NOT reintroduce "Custom software from real work" / "Cast real work into software" / "Custom software, built for your business."

## Product Screens

Screens should look like real software but avoid fake client claims.

- Use neutral sample workflows.
- Show three things only: input, review, output.
- Make the next action obvious.
- Keep metrics believable and clearly sample/demo when not live.

## Motion Rules

- One idea per video.
- 15 seconds first, 30 seconds only when needed.
- No generic dashboard flythroughs.
- Animate messy inputs becoming a usable work screen.
- End with one action: `Send one workflow.`

## Do Not Use

- `AI portal`
- `approved AI`
- `tool sprawl`
- `approval tracker`
- `fake demo`
- Dense module catalogs on public pages
- More than one primary CTA on a screen
