# SuperMega product strategy

## Strategic constraint

SuperMega wins by making two products excellent, configurable, and trustworthy. New ideas enter research or internal tooling until repeated customer evidence justifies a supported Shop or Plant capability.

## Product lifecycle

| Stage | Entry evidence | Product work | Exit gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Named workflow, sponsor, operator, pain, baseline | Map work, risks, data, exceptions, approvals | One bounded problem worth solving |
| Configure | Product and template selected | Configure terminology, roles, fields, thresholds, and sample data | Demo journey accepted |
| Trial candidate | Scope, KPI, users, controls, decision date agreed | Provision isolated workspace and complete activation evidence | Human go decision |
| Managed trial | Every technical and operating gate passed | Guided real use, support, daily briefs, weekly scorecard | Day-30 evidence decision |
| Production | Value, reliability, security, recovery, and ownership accepted | Harden, monitor, support, and document | Stable operating cadence |
| Expand | Repeated value and capacity to support more scope | Add branch, line, module, or integration behind flags and acceptance | Expansion proves incremental value |
| Retire | Low use, replacement, risk, or strategic mismatch | Export, disposition, revoke access, archive evidence | Customer-approved closure |

## Template model

Templates configure a single product; they do not fork it.

- Shop: retail counter, restaurant and inventory, service and bookings, multi-branch control.
- Plant: production job control, quality and traceability, maintenance and downtime, material receiving.

Allowed variation: labels, roles, fields, workflow states, thresholds, approval rules, dashboard composition, imports, and supported connectors. Core identity, audit, accessibility, security, release, and data-isolation contracts remain shared.

## Roadmap

### Now: release and prove

- Promote the consistent `>_` public site and six-route app through verified GitHub workflows.
- Run qualified demos with the correct Shop or Plant template.
- Capture lead, demo, onboarding, support, and day-30 evidence consistently.
- Keep managed writes disabled until the Supabase, identity, RLS, recovery, acceptance, and approval gates pass.

### Next: make trials operational

- Named user access and workspace administration.
- Template configuration, validated import, export, and rollback.
- Durable Shop/Plant state, immutable events, approvals, and audit views.
- Trial scorecard, support queue, evidence export, and operator onboarding.
- Observability, recovery exercises, and incident communications.

### Then: scale what repeats

- Product analytics with privacy and source definitions.
- Supported payment, notification, accounting, procurement, and machine-data connectors selected from customer evidence.
- Governed Assist features: briefs, anomaly triage, drafts, and approval routing.
- Multi-branch and multi-line control with proven isolation and performance.
- Reusable implementation packs, partner delivery, and customer-success automation.

## Feature decision rule

Prioritize a feature only when it improves a core journey, removes a repeated failure, strengthens trust, or proves measurable value for a qualified customer. Require an owner, source evidence, acceptance test, rollout boundary, observability, rollback, support impact, and retirement path.

## Research and development

R&D may explore new models, agent tools, computer use, edge inference, machine integrations, multimodal evidence, and generative interfaces. Experiments stay isolated, use synthetic or approved data, and cannot publish, pay, contact, change access, delete, export, or control equipment without explicit authority. Graduation requires repeat demand and a production-quality control plan.
