# Myanmar conversational commerce product note

Date: 2026-07-30
Decision owner: CEO / Product
Status: source-reviewed local direction; no external action

## Verified signal

The reviewed LinkedIn case describes a Myanmar ecommerce workflow built around how customers already buy: Messenger conversation connected to product, order, inventory, and reporting operations. It reports that the owner needed only brief daily dashboard review during the first five days. Source: [Blingo Ecommerce Platform Uses AI for Myanmar Businesses](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/drphyopaing_ai-ecommerce-myanmar-share-7483054882816675840-XiO2/).

The supplied Instagram posts were inaccessible during review, so they contributed no product or market claim.

## SuperMega product decision

- Keep Ecommerce as the customer storefront and request surface; keep Shop as the only order, stock, payment, refund, tax, and exception authority.
- Treat conversational intake as a future channel adapter into the same governed request contract, not as another product, page, order database, or autonomous seller.
- Require source-message provenance, duplicate-safe recovery, tenant identity, human Shop review, and explicit send authority before any Meta or messaging integration.
- Keep the customer path short: browse or chat, receive a quote, submit one request, track it, and request a correction. Hide internal teams, agents, and operations from customer navigation.

## Pilot measurements

1. Minutes from client catalogue import to a usable demo.
2. Customer request-to-Shop acknowledgement time.
3. Duplicate, correction, and abandoned-request rates.
4. Owner review minutes per day and actions requiring human correction.
5. Order conversion and fulfilment completion, reported only from attributable records.

## Gate

Do not connect Messenger, send customer messages, or claim conversion gains until a founder-approved isolated managed tenant, named operator, privacy review, recovery test, and baseline are present.
