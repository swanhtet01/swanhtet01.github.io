# SuperMega brand system

Version: `cobalt-unboxed-v14-2026-10`

SuperMega builds practical business software for Myanmar. Shop is the only product intended for customer access at launch. The brand should feel clear, capable, and trustworthy through precise product language, calm layouts, and consistent details.

## Identity

- Name: **SuperMega**
- Public domain: `supermega.dev`
- Product domain: `app.supermega.dev`
- Positioning: **Business software for Myanmar.**
- Signature: the standalone **`>_`** mark

The primary lockup is the cobalt `>_` mark beside the exact title-case **SuperMega** wordmark. Keep both unboxed: no rounded-square tile, border, dark backing, descriptor, all-caps treatment, or tagline in the lockup. Keep the mark’s wide, low profile so it reads as a terminal prompt rather than a badge. Use the same geometry and spacing on the company site, Shop site, and app. The wordmark is ink on light surfaces and white on dark surfaces; the `>_` mark stays cobalt in both. The browser-tab icon is the same cobalt mark on a transparent background; do not substitute the older dark tile or a purple variant.

## Core palette

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#ffffff` | Public pages and app shell |
| Raised background | `#f3f6fc` | Quiet section contrast |
| Panel | `#ffffff` | Navigation, cards, and controls |
| Ink | `#151521` | Primary text and wordmark |
| Muted | `#5f6272` | Supporting text |
| Line | `rgba(28, 26, 48, .1)` | Borders |
| Accent | `#2563eb` | `>_` mark, active state, and primary actions |
| Accent strong | `#1d4ed8` | Hover and pressed emphasis |

Do not introduce a separate purple or black-tile identity. Product screens may use meaningful status colors, but the SuperMega mark remains cobalt.

## Typography and wordmark

- Use Geist or Inter with the system sans-serif fallback.
- Set the wordmark as **SuperMega** in the shared Geist/Inter stack, 16px/650 with `-0.02em` tracking. Use ink on light surfaces and white on dark surfaces so contrast changes without changing the identity.
- Keep operational metadata compact and monospace when it improves scanning.
- Do not add a slogan beside the logo. Explain the product on the page, in customer language.

## Voice

- Lead with the work the product helps a business complete.
- Use short, concrete sentences; remove filler and repeated claims.
- Use the company page to state who SuperMega serves and what Shop helps them do. The company hero says **“For Myanmar retailers”** and **“See what sold. Know what needs action.”** Keep the Shop page focused on the complete workflow.
- Name the actual action, owner, or state when it matters.
- Distinguish samples, local trials, and connected customer systems.
- Avoid hype, invented metrics, guaranteed outcomes, and unsupported enterprise claims.

## Interface rules

1. The company site, Shop site, app, and browser-tab icon share the same mark, wordmark, and cobalt accent.
2. Product pages differ by workflow and information, not by a competing logo treatment.
3. Warnings describe real states; they are never decoration.
4. Mobile layouts preserve task priority and required controls.
5. Public samples identify sample records and never imply a live customer system.

## Release checklist

- Use the current unboxed cobalt mark and plain SuperMega wordmark everywhere.
- Describe SuperMega as business software for Myanmar; keep product claims tied to implemented Shop workflows.
- Keep Shop as the customer-facing product while Sites and Commerce remain internal until their access gates change.
- Check keyboard focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, responsive layout, and exact release metadata.
