## The one line

**Custom software at SaaS prices — because we build *with* AI, not despite it. You own it.**

## The thesis (this is the whole company)

SaaS sells you the **average**: one shape of product, built for the median customer in some other country, that you bend your business to fit and rent forever per seat. The hidden bill isn't the subscription — it's the **integration tax** (seven tools that don't talk), the **per-seat tax** (the price grows every time you hire), and the **adaptation tax** (you change how you work to match the software).

For thirty years "custom-built to exactly how you work" was the premium option — too slow and too expensive for an SMB. **AI collapsed that cost.** When AI does the typing, custom stops being the luxury tier. We can build the *exact* thing for less than the 2–3-year total cost of renting the *average* thing.

> **SaaS sells the average. We build the exact. AI made the exact cheaper than the average.**

That is not a feature advantage a competitor patches next quarter. It's an **economic** advantage, and it's the kind that kills categories.

## Why this is true and defensible (the evidence)

- SMBs now run **~25–55 SaaS apps**, adding **~7 net-new per month**, and **over half of paid seats go underused**. Companies underestimate their real SaaS spend by **300%+**. *(Zylo 2025 SaaS Management Index; JumpCloud; Spendesk.)*
- Per-employee SaaS spend averages **~$4,830/yr** and is rising fastest at SMBs. A 15-person shop on POS + accounting + HR SaaS clears **$7k+ over 2–3 years** before any per-seat growth.
- A one-time custom build + a light care plan wins that math **and** removes the integration tax — one system instead of seven that fight each other.

## The five UVPs (page-ready, all true)

1. **Custom software at SaaS prices.** We build with AI, so bespoke costs what off-the-shelf used to.
2. **You own it — no per-seat tax.** It doesn't get more expensive every time you hire. No vendor can switch it off.
3. **AI-native, not AI-bolted-on.** AI is the substrate — every module reads, drafts, classifies, and reasons. Not a chatbot stapled to a CRUD app.
4. **Built for Myanmar.** MMK, KBZPay / WavePay / MMQR, MY/EN, and it keeps working when the power and the internet don't.
5. **One system, not seven subscriptions.** Your tools stop fighting each other; your data lives in one place you control.

## What "AI-native" means here (so it isn't a buzzword)

- **Built by AI** → custom economics. The reason we can quote a number an SMB will say yes to.
- **Runs on AI** → every build can read messy real inputs (chat photos, scanned forms, Burmese names, MMQR slips), draft the next action, and explain itself — with a human approval gate on anything that sends, pays, or changes the books.
- **Improves with AI** → each client build feeds the module library, so the next client is faster and cheaper. (The flywheel — see [MONEY-MACHINE.md](./MONEY-MACHINE.md).)

## What we are NOT (hold this line)

- Not a generic SaaS with a chatbot tab.
- Not a dev shop that hands you a repo and a goodbye. We hand you a **running thing at a live URL**, and a care plan if you want it.
- Not an ad agency / social-calendar shop. (That's the margin trap `.creative` explicitly avoids.)
- Not a fake demo. Everything we show publicly is real and reachable, or clearly labelled as a guided preview. *(Standing rule.)*

## The category claim

We are a **custom build studio for anything** — software, internal tools, dashboards, lead-gen, LMS, POS, automations, AI agents, and hardware / IoT — that is AI-native, built from your real (messy) work, local-first, compliance-ready, and **yours to keep**. The competitor SaaS is the average. We're the exact. That's the kill.

Plain, confident, bilingual MY/EN. No hype, no fake numbers. We state the honest line — *"no Myanmar wallet hands you a clean API; our value is absorbing that pain so you see a working thing"* — because that honesty **is** the differentiator. Studio line: **"Make it beautiful. Then make it run."**
