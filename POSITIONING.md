# SuperMega positioning

## The one line

**SuperMega builds Shop and Plant—operating software for Myanmar businesses.**

## What customers buy

| Product | Customer problem | First value | Expansion path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shop** | Daily sales, stock, purchasing, bookings, payments, close, and exceptions live in disconnected records | One clear daily workflow and owner view | Multi-branch standards, approvals, integrations, and governed assistance |
| **Plant** | Jobs, machines, materials, quality, maintenance, and shift handoffs are hard to trace | One production queue with visible state, issue ownership, and handoff | Lines, planning, traceability, maintenance intelligence, and governed assistance |

## Ideal first customers

- Owner-led Myanmar businesses with a repeat daily workflow and a measurable operational problem.
- A named sponsor, an operator who will use the system, and a bounded location, branch, or line.
- Willingness to establish a baseline, use approved data, and make a day-30 decision from evidence.

Do not lead with broad transformation, autonomous control, or an unbounded replacement program. Lead with one workflow worth proving.

## Differentiation

- Local operating context and implementation support.
- Product templates that adapt without fragmenting the core platform.
- Evidence-first workflows, named ownership, approvals, and immutable operational history.
- AI assistance inside governed work—not a detached chatbot and not unsupervised authority.
- A clear path from isolated demo to controlled managed trial.

## Proof hierarchy

1. Product demo with realistic sample state.
2. Qualified workflow map and baseline.
3. Bounded managed-trial plan and control evidence.
4. Customer-approved operational results and limitations.
5. Expansion decision tied to measurable value and risk.

## Calls to action

- Public product page: **Open the Shop demo** or **Open the Plant demo**.
- Qualified lead: **Map the workflow**.
- After demo: **Prove this workflow for 30 days**.

Commercial, security, compliance, performance, and outcome claims require current evidence and human approval. The detailed lead, demo, onboarding, and follow-up system is `SUPERMEGA_LAUNCH_AND_TRIAL_PLAYBOOK.md`.
